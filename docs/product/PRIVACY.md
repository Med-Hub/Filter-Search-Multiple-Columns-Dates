# Filter & Search privacy notice

Prepared 1 November 2026. Applies to Filter & Search – Multiple Columns & Dates and its product support.

## Who is responsible

Veilig Starten is responsible for personal information received for product support. Our address is Groenewoudseweg 173, 5621 BG Eindhoven, Netherlands. Contact **mmohsine@veiligstarten.nl** for support or privacy requests.

## Information processed inside Power BI

The visual uses the record identifiers, selected search values, dates and search queries supplied within your Power BI report to find matching records and apply filters. The reviewed visual does not directly transmit those report values or queries to Veilig Starten and does not include its own analytics or advertising telemetry.

Your organisation controls the report, dataset, access permissions and report retention. Power BI may save filters with the report. Microsoft and your organisation process information under their own arrangements; this notice does not replace their privacy information.

Date Filter Pro uses Power BI's licensing interface to determine whether the signed-in user has a usable matching plan. Microsoft handles Marketplace transactions and licence assignment. The visual has no publisher payment form and does not collect payment-card details.

## When you contact support

We receive your email address, any name and organisation you provide, your message and attachments. We use this information to answer questions, investigate faults and follow up on related issues. Providing contact and problem details is voluntary, but we may be unable to help without enough information to understand and respond to your request.

Please send synthetic examples and cropped screenshots. Do not send passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information or confidential reports. We remove unnecessary personal information from attachments and use synthetic reproductions when investigating issues.

We use the following legal grounds:

- **Contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR:** where support is necessary to perform a contract with you as an individual, or to take requested steps before entering that contract.
- **Legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f):** answering enquiries from other users and company representatives, fixing product faults, maintaining a limited support history for follow-up and protecting the service against misuse. We limit information, access and retention and consider objections individually.
- **Legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c):** where a specific applicable law requires us to retain or disclose particular records.

Support correspondence is not used for advertising or automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. It is not sold or added to marketing lists.

## Who receives support information

Within Veilig Starten, only the owner reads product-support correspondence. We use **Zoho Mail** to receive, store and send email. Zoho and its relevant service providers may process this information to operate, secure and support that service. We may disclose necessary records to competent authorities where legally required or to professional advisers when needed to handle a specific legal claim, subject to applicable confidentiality obligations.

Our current Zoho Mail account uses Zoho's US service domain. Zoho identifies this domain as its United States data centre. Zoho group entities and service providers may also provide support from other countries, including India. This notice does not claim that support emails remain exclusively in the EU.

Zoho describes its data-processing agreements and Standard Contractual Clauses as safeguards for relevant international transfers. Its [privacy information](https://www.zoho.com/privacy.html), [privacy FAQ](https://www.zoho.com/privacy/privacy-faq.html) and [subprocessor list](https://www.zoho.com/privacy/sub-processors.html) explain its arrangements. Contact us to request information about the safeguards applicable to your support correspondence.

## How long information is kept

We keep ordinary support correspondence while resolving an issue and, where needed for follow-up questions or recurring problems, for up to **12 months after resolution**. We delete it sooner when it is no longer needed. Attachments containing personal data are deleted once they are no longer necessary for investigation.

If particular records are needed to meet a legal obligation or handle a specific legal dispute, we retain only those necessary records for that purpose and delete them when that need ends. This does not extend retention of all support emails.

Deletion covers active copies under our control. Zoho states that deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for up to three months; backup copies are subject to its deletion cycle. Your Power BI report retention is controlled by your organisation, not this support-email policy.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email **mmohsine@veiligstarten.nl**. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity. We respond within the time limits required by applicable law.

You may complain to the Dutch [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/) or another competent supervisory authority. For personal data in your Power BI report, contact the organisation that controls that report.
