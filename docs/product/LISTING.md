**Summary:** Search text and numbers across columns. Add date conditions with Date Filter Pro.

**Keywords:** multi-column search; text filter; date filter

**Description**

Find records across multiple columns with one compact search visual for Power BI. Search names, suppliers, codes and numeric values without adding a separate search box for every column.

Text and numeric search are free. Choose Contains, Starts with or Exact matching, search any selected field or every selected field, and control whether accents are ignored. Codes stored as text retain their leading zeros.

Date Filter Pro lets you apply one date condition to a chosen date column: on a day, between dates, on or before, on or after, or blank dates. Combine that date condition with text search on the same record. Date filtering requires a separate Date Filter Pro per-user subscription managed by Microsoft and a license assigned to the signed-in Power BI user. Installing the visual does not assign a paid license.

To set up the visual, supply a unique record key and search/date columns from the same table. Use a distinct key for each delivery or transaction, even when several rows share one article number. Add ordinary Date or Date/Time columns rather than date hierarchies.

The visual supports up to 10 search fields and 5 date fields, with a limit of 100,000 candidate rows and additional safeguards for data size. Queries matching more than 5,000 records must be narrowed. Saved report filters can be cleared through the visual; restoring a bookmark does not promise full restoration of the original search text and options.

Use a distinct record key to keep separate transactions searchable even when they share the same article number. For setup help, contact mmohsine@veiligstarten.nl.
